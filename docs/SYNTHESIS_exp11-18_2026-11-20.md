# exp11, exp12, exp17 and exp18 together: what is ruled out, what is open, the next experiment (2026-11-20)

The fourth of the operator's four prompts. Read with SE-R3 and `docs/SYNTHESIS_exp14-17_2026-11-20.md`. The
published ratio is about (1 + b/x)/2, so its 2x bar asks for the learned burst synapse b at about 3x the background
x.

## What the four say together

- **exp11 and exp12:** three ways of routing inhibition land within 0.08 of each other, each 0/8.
- **exp17:** burst-synapse selectivity on new seeds 9-16 is >= 2.0 on 7/8, above its own start on 8/8, and above
  1000 relabelings on 8/8. The shuffled run, with the same bursts and no fixed groups, stays at 1.14.
- **exp18:** a threshold that blocked 91% of the noise synapses' potentiation left the mean ratio at 1.839 in both
  arms (0/8 each, not kept) and lowered selectivity on 8/8 seeds.

Across them, no change to competition, forgetting or potentiation timing has carried the ratio past 2. The ceiling
the prompts asked about did not break. It also was never a ceiling on learning (SE-R3): selectivity is at about 2.5
where the ratio is at 1.84.

## Ruled out, in this network and by this yardstick

1. **The sharpness of competition** (exp11, exp12). Only direct inhibition was re-tested on selectivity (SE-R3).
2. **Forgetting and consolidation** (exp8-10) and **multiplicative homeostasis** (exp8's reasoning), as before.
3. **Burst energy alone as the source of the selectivity.** The shuffled null has the same bursts and stays at 1.14
   (exp17). The structure needs the fixed groups.
4. **Saturation as what held the claimed synapses back in exp18.** No claimed burst synapse came near wmax = 2.0
   (largest 1.38; post hoc, `exp18_posthoc_out.txt`).

## Open

1. **Whether chance-coincidence potentiation of the noise synapses holds the ratio near 1.9.** exp18 blocked 91%
   of it and the ratio held, but not by the arithmetic of the score. The noise synapses' lost weight went to the
   budget; the silent burst synapses, which only normalisation moves, rose 18.4%. Had the claimed burst synapses
   kept pace, the mean ratio would have been 1.997 instead of 1.840 (post hoc). They rose 6.9%. The gate also
   blocked 4.5% of their own potentiation events. So exp18 cannot say whether noise potentiation holds the ratio;
   a gate on the noise synapses alone would.
2. **What holds the claimed burst synapses near 3x the background.** The leading candidate is untested: the claimer
   fires inside its own group's volley, so the volley's later spikes depress what its earlier spikes potentiated.
   exp18's lag (point 1) has two readings that exp18 cannot separate: that equilibrium, or the gate's own trimming
   of the claimed synapses' potentiation.
3. **Behaviour.** No experiment has replayed each group's burst with the weights frozen to see whether its claimer,
   and only its claimer, answers. Weight selectivity is shown (exp17); response selectivity is not.
4. **Whether exp11's and exp12's topologies show the same selectivity.** They were not re-tested (SE-R3).
5. **A lower background rate,** the third named redesign. Untested.
6. **The threshold lowered selectivity on 8/8 seeds.** Descriptively this is the same lag as point 1: the other
   groups' burst synapses rose 15.8%, close to the budget, and the claimed group's 6.9%. Why the claimed synapses
   lagged is point 2.

## The next single experiment: exp19, bar set now

**Tighten the volley: burst jitter sd 0.5 ms instead of 2 ms, nothing else changed.**
- It tests open point 2 directly.
- It changes the input, not the plasticity rule, which is what prompt one asked for. A tighter volley also raises
  the peak drive, so the pre-registration's Amendment 1 adds the measures that tell timing from drive.

The bar is in `prereg/PREREG_exp19_volley_jitter.md`, committed with this synthesis, NOT YET RUN. In short:
- **Supported:** (a AND b) on at least 6 of 8 seeds, selectivity not lower, and the mechanism's own sign: fewer
  depression events on the claimer's own burst synapses per own-group burst, on at least 6 of 8 seeds.
- **Refuted:** the timing moves but the bar does not.
- **A regime guard comes first.**

If it is refuted, open point 3, the frozen-weight replay, is next.

## Corrected after an outside read (2026-11-20, SE-R4)

The first version of this synthesis, pushed the same day, said four things that do not hold, and exp19's
pre-registration a fifth (SE-R4):
- under Ruled out, "So any change that only moves the noise synapses leaves the ratio where it was";
- that the docstring's diagnosis "that spurious noise LTP holds the ratio near 1.9x, does not survive";
- that the lag read "as if the claimed synapses were pinned by their own timing rather than by the budget";
- with the README, normalisation raised the silent synapses "so the top and bottom of the ratio rose together";
- exp19's pre-registration, that exp18 "ruled out the noise synapses as what holds the published ratio" (its
  Amendment 1 corrects it; the original stays, as a pre-registration must).

Gemini, signed out, read the pull request and said the ruled-out list could not tell masking from absence, and
that the timing reading was stated too strongly. The post hoc check it led to (`exp18_posthoc.py`) shows the ratio
would have risen to 1.997 had the claimed synapses followed the budget. The top and bottom of the ratio did not
rise; both stayed where they were (split means 0.934 over 0.508 in the control, 0.934 over 0.510 under the
threshold). So the first and fourth statements are false, and the second, third and fifth are stronger than exp18
allows. SE-R4.

Gemini's other alternative, saturation near wmax, does not hold against the weights; nor does its claim that the
ratio's flatness is "an arithmetic artifact" of normalisation.
