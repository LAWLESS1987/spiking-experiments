# What the ratio measures after exp14-17, and what it cannot see (2026-11-20)

Written before exp18's plan (`prereg/PREREG_exp18_ltp_threshold.md`) as its diagnosis. Sources: the exp14-17 logs,
`exp16_posthoc2_out.txt`, `exp17_out.txt`, and a split of exp17's saved weights into the ratio's parts
(`exp17_decompose_ratio.py`, output `exp17_decompose_out.txt`). Read against the retractions SE-R3 and SE-R2.

## 1. What the ratio measures now

**Its definition** (exp11's scoring lines, exp16's `score()`): for each group, the claiming output's mean weight over
the group's 20 inputs, each input counted with both of its synapses (burst and noise), over the same output's mean on
inputs 60-99, whose burst synapses never receive a spike; averaged over the three groups.

**Its parts**, on exp17's eight new seeds (real run, 24 groups): the claimed burst synapse b = 0.696; the noise
synapses of the same inputs 0.234; on inputs 60-99 the silent burst synapses 0.238 and the noise synapses 0.237. So
the per-group ratio is (b + n)/(s + n), about (1 + b/x)/2 with x ~ 0.237 the unlearned background. Each learned
synapse is averaged with an unlearned one: a ratio of 2 needs b at about 3x the background. The runs sit at b/x =
2.95, ratio 1.97 per group (1.74-2.40; 10 of 24 groups above 2.0).

**What moves it** is any burst. It is 1.07 at t = 0, before learning, because the claimer is picked from the same
weights; the change is in the burst synapses (mean |dw| 0.144 against 0.053 for the noise synapses, exp16). Bursts
with no fixed groups (exp14's shuffled null) still reach 1.64 against 1.84. On exp17's seeds the shuffled run's
claimed and other-group burst weights are 0.508 and 0.462: growth without selectivity, ratio 1.67. The ratio mostly
reads "the bursting inputs grew and the silent ones did not", halved by the noise synapse in its numerator; the fixed
groups add about 0.2. Per-output normalisation rescales an output's weights by one factor, so it leaves the ratio
unchanged at each step (exp8's reasoning, SE-R3).

## 2. What a pattern-learning signal looks like that the ratio cannot see

- **A contrast inside the bursting inputs.** The claimer's burst weight on its own group against its burst weight on
  the other two (exp17's selectivity): 1.12-1.24 at t = 0, then 2.78, 2.85, 2.67, 2.33, 1.87, 2.18, 2.79, 2.57 on
  seeds 9-16, against 1.11-1.20 shuffled, and above 1000 random relabelings on 8 of 8. The ratio cannot see this: its
  denominator holds no bursting input, so an output that strengthened all sixty bursting inputs alike would score as
  high while selecting nothing.
- **Distinct claims.** Seed 13, the one selectivity miss (1.87), is the seed whose claims are [0, 2, 0].
- **Behaviour, not weights.** With plasticity frozen, replay each group's burst alone: a learned pattern drives its
  own output and leaves the other two quiet; a burst detector answers all three. No experiment in exp1-17 runs this.
- **The distribution, not the count.** On the same eight runs the ratio reads 1.85-2.08 while selectivity spreads
  from 1.87 to 2.85. The ~1.9 sits in the yardstick (SE-R3); selectivity shows no ceiling there.

## 3. One mechanistic claim that would make 2x reachable without changing the plasticity rule (UNTESTED)

**The claimed burst synapses stop near 3x the background because the claimer fires inside its own group's volley.**
A group's 20 spikes arrive with a 2 ms jitter, spread over about 8 ms. As the claimed weights grow, the claimer
crosses its threshold earlier in the volley, so a growing share of the same group's spikes arrive after the post
spike and are depressed (Apost = -1.05 Apre). Growth stops where the volley's potentiation and depression balance.

If true, a tighter volley moves more of each burst before the post spike. That is a change to the input, with the
rule untouched. b/x then passes 3, the ratio passes 2, and selectivity rises with it. If false, tightening the volley
moves neither. Not run.

## Limits

One architecture (direct inhibition). The split reads weights, not behaviour. Claim 3 is a hypothesis with a test
named, not a result.
